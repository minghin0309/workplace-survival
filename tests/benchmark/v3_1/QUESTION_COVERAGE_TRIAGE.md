# v3 Required-Question Coverage Triage

## Confirmed outcome

The first v3 holdout failed only the preregistered question-coverage gates:

- required question concepts: 2, minimum 3;
- cases with required questions: 2, minimum 3.

Revision coverage, accepted-turn coverage, and uncertainty coverage passed. The Skill was never executed.

## Why the four material-information designs did not yield four required questions

### V3-004 — direct responsibility defect dominated the unknown

The draft presented an unowned commercial concession as already settled. Independent gold therefore had enough evidence to assign responsibility Red and require removal/rerouting without first asking who held approval.

Construction lesson:

- an unknown authority does not force a question when the visible commitment is already unsafe;
- a question candidate must not contain a separate direct Red defect that can be repaired without the missing answer.

### V3-005 — successful question candidate

The recipient explicitly needed a completion time, the time was genuinely absent, and the response could not safely satisfy the request without it. Gold required `confirmed-deadline`.

Construction lesson:

- the unknown must block the message's main purpose;
- the recipient's requirement and the missing fact must both be explicit.

### V3-006 — no two-of-three question majority

Labelers agreed the message had timing/impact weaknesses but disagreed whether a question was mandatory, whether placeholders were enough, and which revision concept governed the fix. No required question or revision concept reached adjudication majority.

Construction lesson:

- generic uncertainty is insufficient;
- the case must define one irreducible decision dependency rather than several loosely related gaps;
- placeholder-only completion must be explicitly unsafe or impossible.

### V3-007 — adjudicated Green

The draft could safely report the observed figure while leaving the decision with the manager. Missing provenance was useful but not required to complete the communication under the supplied facts.

Construction lesson:

- provenance is question-required only when the quantitative claim cannot be safely qualified or omitted;
- a candidate fails if a safe evidence-bounded rewrite can proceed without the answer.

## Remediation principles

v3.1 will overprovision six independent question candidates so one disputed judgement cannot invalidate coverage.

Every candidate must satisfy all of these construction predicates:

1. one named material concept is absent from every SUT-visible field;
2. an explicit recipient requirement or decision depends on that concept;
3. the message cannot be safely finalized with qualification, omission, or a placeholder;
4. supplying the answer enables a complete safe message;
5. removing the dependency makes the question unnecessary;
6. no independent direct Red contradiction, unsafe commitment, accusation, or recipient-scope defect dominates the unknown;
7. each candidate exercises one primary unknown, not a bundle of unrelated gaps.

These predicates are construction gates only. Independent gold remains authoritative and may still disagree.
